What is Laya deciding?

The engine protects the next piece's spawn and stack headroom, then filters holes and strictly worse outcomes. Laya chooses among the surviving legal landings. A single safe option is labeled forced; an inferior option is never added just to offer a choice.

Laya aligns the current piece while planning two or three future placements in sequence. It uses normal gravity until that reserve is ready. Every queued placement is checked against the predicted board; a mismatch invalidates dependent plans.

Probabilities come from Laya; board effects come from the game. The optional pause affects the current decision only, never reserve building. Escape returns control to you.
