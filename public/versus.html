How survival planning works

Each AI aligns the current piece first and uses its normal fall time to plan a chain of two or three upcoming placements. It only accelerates the descent when that many moves are ready. Gravity can still lock a piece naturally before the queue fills.

Each decision sees the board produced by the preceding placement, including line clears. Ready moves are consumed in order and the reserve is replenished. Actual board, piece, turn, and reachability must still match; stale dependent plans are discarded.

The engine filters next-spawn collisions, preserves headroom when possible, and removes inferior landings. The selected model chooses within that safe shortlist. The panel labels a sole safe choice as forced. This is planning a conditional sequence, not a guarantee of survival.
